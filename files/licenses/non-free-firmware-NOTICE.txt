Non-free firmware notice
========================

The released rootfs image contains binary firmware blobs from the Debian
non-free-firmware component, installed by these packages:

  firmware-realtek        20250410-2
  firmware-mediatek       20250410-2
  firmware-atheros        20250410-2
  firmware-misc-nonfree   20250410-2

These blobs are redistributable, but they are not free software: their
licenses are vendor binary-redistribution terms, are not OSI-approved and
some forbid reverse engineering. They are required by the USB WiFi adapters
the board supports (rtl8xxxu / rtw88 / mt76 / mt7601u / rt2800usb /
ath9k_htc), so they are part of the release.

The complete per-blob license texts and copyright notices are shipped with
each package inside the image at:
  /usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright
